Learn exactly what to say in every position of the prayer, one step at a time.
The Arabic, how to pronounce it in your own language, and what it means, side by side.
No account needed: just you and the words of the salah.

You know the movements. Standing, bowing, forehead to the ground, sitting. What you do not know is what to say while you are down there, and nobody teaches it slowly enough to actually learn.

FOURTEEN STEPS, IN ORDER

Every position of the prayer, from the opening takbir to the final salam, in the order you perform them. Tap one and you get four things, never mixed together: what your body does, the Arabic text, how to pronounce it written in your own language, and what it actually means.

LINE BY LINE

Long passages such as Al-Fatiha and the Tashahhud are broken line by line, with the Arabic, the pronunciation and the meaning side by side on each line. That alignment is the whole lesson. You learn which words you are saying, not just the sound of them.

HEAR IT, THEN HEAR YOURSELF

For Al-Fatiha and the short surah, tap play and hear Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary or Mishary Rashid Alafasy recite the line. Al-Husary's teaching recording is slow and detached, made to be repeated after.

Then record yourself. Both traces are drawn on the same waveform, so you can see where you rushed and where you stopped short. The recording stays on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere.

WRITTEN FOR YOUR LANGUAGE

The pronunciation is not transliterated once and reused. A French reader does not read "Subhaana" the way an English reader does, so each language gets its own spelling, written for the way that language is read aloud. Fourteen languages: English, French, Arabic, Indonesian, Malay, Urdu, Bengali, Turkish, Russian, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian and Dutch.

THE WHOLE QUR'AN, VERSE BY VERSE

A new tab lets you swipe through the Qur'an one verse at a time, with a reciter, a translation in your language, the verses you love and your own private notes.

A WIDGET THAT KEEPS TEACHING

Add the widget to your Home Screen and the positions cycle on their own, with what is said at each one. You are not studying. You are just seeing it, several times a day, until it sticks. The widget is part of Salat Premium.

PRIVATE, AND THE PRAYER WORKS OFFLINE

No account. No sign-in. Your progress and your voice recordings stay on your iPhone. The lessons of the prayer, their text and their reciters are inside the app, so they work on a plane, in a basement, anywhere. Only the recitation of the Qur'an is downloaded, verse by verse, as you listen. The free version shows an occasional ad at a natural pause and sends anonymous usage statistics; the privacy policy says exactly what.

ON THE CONTENT

The prayer described here is the ground shared by the four Sunni schools of law. Where they differ (the wording of the Tashahhud, where the hands rest, the Qunut), the app tells you they differ, in a note on that step, instead of quietly picking one.

It is a memorisation aid. It does not replace a teacher, and it does not claim to.
