Learn Salah teaches you exactly what to say in every position of the prayer, one step at a time.

The Arabic, how to pronounce it in your own language, and what it means, side by side. No account needed: just you and the words of the salah.

You know the movements. Standing, bowing, forehead to the ground, sitting. What you do not know is what to say while you are down there, and nobody teaches it slowly enough. Learn Salah does.

LEARN SALAH IN FOURTEEN STEPS
Every position of the prayer, from the opening takbir to the final salam, in the order you perform them. Tap one and you get four things, never mixed together: what your body does, the Arabic text, the pronunciation in your own language, and the meaning.

LINE BY LINE
Long passages such as Al-Fatiha and the Tashahhud are broken down line by line, with the Arabic, the pronunciation and the meaning for each line.

LISTEN AND REPEAT
Hear each line, repeat it, and drill the passages until they stay.

FOR BEGINNERS AND REVERTS
Whether you are new to Islam or never learned properly as a child, learn salah at your own pace, offline.

Learn salah once, pray with confidence for life.
