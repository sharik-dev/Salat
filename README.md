# Salat

An iOS app that teaches **what to say during the prayer**: the Arabic, its
pronunciation written the way *you* read, and its meaning.

Two ways to learn:

- **Learn** — the 14 steps of the prayer, each with the posture, the Arabic
  line by line, the phonetic transcription and the translation.
- **Practice** — the same steps on a loop, plus a **home screen widget** that
  cycles through the positions on its own, so it sinks in without effort.

Illustrations are faceless silhouettes; the content is the ground shared by the
four Sunni schools, with points of difference noted rather than settled.

English, French and Arabic. Adding a language is two JSON files.

See `CLAUDE.md` for the architecture and the rules the project holds itself to.
